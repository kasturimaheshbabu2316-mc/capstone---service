# VIP Customer Support Policy

Ola provides white-glove support services to passengers enrolled in Ola Select and Prime Plus tiers. VIP support tickets automatically skip standard automated routing and assign directly to dedicated Senior Relationship Managers. First-response SLAs for VIP inquiries are capped at 15 minutes across all severity categories with guaranteed resolution within 6 hours. In the event of a vehicle breakdown during a VIP journey, Ola dispatches a replacement Prime luxury cab at zero additional fare within 10 minutes.
