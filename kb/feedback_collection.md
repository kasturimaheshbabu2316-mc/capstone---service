Ola aggregates passenger and partner performance feedback through structured post-ride evaluation prompts. Immediately within 60 seconds of trip completion, riders and drivers receive an in-app prompt to submit a 1-to-5 star rating alongside optional defect tags. Ratings of 1 or 2 stars trigger an automated customer care callback inquiry within 4 hours to capture root-cause telemetry. Quarterly Net Promoter Score (NPS) surveys are sampled across frequent riders to gauge long-term service satisfaction and driver behavioral trends.
