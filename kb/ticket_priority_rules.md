# Ticket Priority Rules

Ola categorizes customer support tickets into four distinct priority tiers: P1 (Critical), P2 (High), P3 (Medium), and P4 (Low). P1 tickets are strictly reserved for active passenger safety incidents, medical emergencies, and vehicular accidents occurring during a ride. P2 tickets cover in-transit payment failures, driver fraud allegations, and severe app navigation breakdowns. P3 handles standard billing disputes, fare miscalculations, and ride receipt requests. P4 is designated for general inquiries, lost item tracking, and non-urgent feedback submissions.
