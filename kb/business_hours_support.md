# Business Hours and Operational Support

Ola maintains differentiated operational coverage across support categories. Passenger emergency support and active ride safety desks operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year without interruption. General inquiry handling, billing dispute reviews, and driver partner physical onboarding centers operate Monday through Saturday from 09:00 to 18:00 Indian Standard Time. On national holidays, non-critical support tickets transition to on-call duty managers with extended response SLAs of 24 additional hours.
