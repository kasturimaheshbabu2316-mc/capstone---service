Ola prioritizes chronic complaints through automated recurrence detection algorithms. When a customer logs three or more complaints regarding the same ride issue or driver partner within a 14-day rolling window, the system flags the ticket as a Chronic Repeat Defect. Such repeat tickets bypass standard frontline queues and route immediately to a Senior Quality Supervisor within 2 hours of submission. Supervisors must schedule an outbound telephonic review and conduct driver partner fleet audit within 24 hours.
