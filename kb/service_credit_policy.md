Ola issues service credits in the form of Ola Money cashback balances and ride discount coupons for minor service deficiencies. Delays exceeding 20 minutes attributable to dispatch errors qualify for a ₹50 service voucher valid for 30 days. AC non-functioning complaints in Prime Sedan or Prime SUV rides result in an automatic ₹100 Ola Money credit upon telemetry review. Service credits are instantly deposited into the customer's wallet within 24 hours of ticket validation and cannot be converted into cash withdrawals.
