# Customer Support Data Retention Policy

Ola governs the lifecycle of customer support records under stringent regulatory privacy standards. Active customer tickets and chat transcripts are retained in live production stores for 180 days following ticket resolution. Thereafter, personally identifiable contact numbers and ride coordinates are redacted and archived into encrypted cold storage for 3 years to comply with national transportation audit mandates. Customers may request complete account data erasure under statutory right-to-be-forgotten guidelines, with deletion executed within 30 days of verified identity proof.
