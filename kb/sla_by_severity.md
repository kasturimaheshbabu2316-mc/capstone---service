# SLA by Severity Policy

Ola enforces strict resolution Service Level Agreements based on ticket severity levels. Sev-1 safety incidents require a mandatory first response within 15 minutes and complete operational resolution within 4 hours. Sev-2 high-priority issues require an initial acknowledgement within 1 hour and resolution within 12 hours. Sev-3 standard inquiries mandate response within 4 hours and resolution within 24 hours. Sev-4 low-priority items must receive an initial response within 12 hours and final closure within 48 hours.
