Ola adheres to proactive communication protocols during platform technical disruptions. When an API gateway failure or geolocation outage affects more than 5 percent of active bookings, the Central Incident Management Team initiates incident status alerts within 15 minutes. Passenger notifications are transmitted via in-app banners, push notifications, and broadcast SMS alerts detailing estimated time to recovery. Post-mortem root cause incident reports are published on the Ola Operations Transparency Portal within 48 hours of system recovery.
