# Incident Escalation Matrix

Unresolved support tickets follow Ola's three-tier organizational escalation matrix. Frontline Tier-1 customer representatives own ticket resolution for the initial 2 hours following creation. If an issue breaches its milestone threshold or requires specialized approval, the ticket automatically escalates to a Tier-2 Support Lead for investigation. Cases remaining unresolved after 8 hours or involving legal liability escalate directly to the Tier-3 City Operations Head for final executive determination.
