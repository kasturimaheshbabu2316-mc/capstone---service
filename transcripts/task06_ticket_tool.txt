Formula: S_esc = w1 * float(escalated) + w2 * (days_since_created / 30)
Weights: w1 = 0.6 (escalation flag), w2 = 0.4 (recency aging over 30 days)
Refinement: Recency aging term zeroed for 'Resolved' and 'Closed' tickets.

C. Threshold Justification:
  • ESCALATION_THRESHOLD is set to tau = 0.4400, matching the empirical 80th percentile of scores.
  • At tau = 0.4400, all explicitly escalated tickets (w1 * 1.0 = 0.60 >= 0.4400) trigger recommend_escalation = True.
  • An unescalated ticket at the 80th percentile of age (23.0 days) scores 0.4 * (23.0/30) = 0.3067 < 0.4400.
  • This guarantees typical aging does not generate false alarms, while flagging the top 20% highest risk tickets.
